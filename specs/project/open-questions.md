# Open questions and future features

Recorded: 2026-10-04. Keep undecided ideas separate from first-release requirements.

## Deliberately open

- **Vocabulary selection:** no formal method is required now. The owner will select important meanings manually and gather learner feedback before choosing a special approach. Do not repeatedly block content creation on this question.
- **English levels:** exact target ranges, course labels, and how course difficulty should be assessed remain open. The intended beginner audience already has basic English.
- **AI assistance:** AI suggestions for subtitle vocabulary and level estimates were discussed, but no selection or automatic approval feature was agreed.
- **Multiple level versions:** separate course versions or learning paths for different levels were discussed but not approved.
- **Future review method:** the owner may use a method similar to an app they called "memorize". The exact app and desired behavior are unconfirmed. Do not assume a particular spaced-review algorithm.
