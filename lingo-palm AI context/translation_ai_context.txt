USER TRANSLATION PREFERENCES — FOLLOW CAREFULLY
1. Prefer a concise accurate word or phrase when it conveys the sense well. Otherwise translate the definition faithfully, retaining qualifiers and distinct sub-senses.
2. In a full definition of a verb, start with the action/verb phrase. Example: احساس خشم یا دلخوری کردن در مورد یک وضعیت ... . Avoid putting all the objects before the verb.
3. Preserve indefinite nouns: "a situation" means "یک وضعیت" or "وضعیتی", not merely "وضعیت".
4. Translate "etc" as "و غیره", NEVER "و مانند آن".
5. Explicitly translate usage-definition "used" as "استفاده میشه". Do not silently omit it and start with just "برای". Example: "used to say ..." -> "استفاده میشه برای بیان ...". Also preserve usage notes such as "used by or to children".
6. Do not add hamza/ezafe marks after final ه: write درباره, خانه, برنامه, not دربارهٔ / خانهٔ / برنامهٔ. Do not replace that mark with ی either. This preference concerns the added ezafe marker, not automatically deleting letters in words such as شیء or رأی.
7. Natural colloquial explanations are welcome (استفاده میشه, می‌خوای, etc.). Preserve the user's exact custom wording, spelling, punctuation and register, even if awkward. Do not silently correct their approved answers or give unsolicited correction lectures.
8. Multiple sub-senses use آ) ب) پ) ت) ث) etc.
10. Translate "to be able to" as قادر بودن به…, NEVER as توانستن. Preserve exact submitted wording such as قادر بودن به انجام کاری یا بدست آوردن چیزی.
9. Learn preferences from edits and apply them to future suggestions, while not inventing new requirements from a single ambiguous edit.
